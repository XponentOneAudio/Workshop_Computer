Pitch
A copy of the card's engine running in this browser, playing the faders on screen: for shaping a sound without the module, or before sending it. The slider is the Main knob (C1 to C7, then SEMI and FINE); the keys A–K play C4 to C5 (W E T Y U the black notes, Z X an octave down or up). The X and Y knobs are the depths and ratios already on the MUTANT pages.

Presets

    A preset is all eight pages of faders (which include the depths, ratios, modes and wave the knobs set; the pitch stays on the knob). Presets live in this browser. Loading one sends it to the card if one is connected. The card itself keeps nothing over power-off.

    Inputs & outputs

    Inputs
    CV In 1Pitch, 1 V/oct, added to Main
    CV In 2Modulation, as the MOD page sets (at first: mutant 1's depth, +5 V for all of it)
    Audio In 1Hard sync from another oscillator, while patched: the wave restarts at each rising zero crossing
    Audio In 2Audio-rate modulation of the depths (MOD page, faders 5 and 6)
    Pulse In 1Restarts the wave
    Pulse In 2Restarts both LFOs
    Outputs
    Audio Out 1The oscillator, after both mutants
    Audio Out 2OSC page fader 6: the same, the dry wave, after mutant 1 only, or a square an octave or two down
    CV Out 1, 2LFO 1, 2, ±5 V
    Pulse Out 1LFO 1's square
    Pulse Out 2The oscillator's square: a sync out for another oscillator

    8mu

    The 8mu can plug into the module (no computer needed) or into this computer, which passes it on to the card.

    ButtonsA Mutant 1, B Mutant 2, C Osc, D LFO 1. Press the same button again for its second page: A Warp 1, B Warp 2, C Mod, D LFO 2.
    MutantMode, ratio, depth, window, feedback, mix, LFO 1 → depth, LFO 2 → depth.
    WarpEight points of a curve that pushes the phase back and forth, for that mutant's WARP mode.
    OscWave, semitones, fine, bit crush, drive, Out 2, glide, level.
    LFORate, shape, then where it goes: ratio 1, ratio 2, wave, pitch, mix 1, mix 2.
    ModCV In 2 → depth 1, depth 2, ratio 1, ratio 2; Audio In 2 → depth 1, depth 2; tilt → depth 1 (left-right), depth 2 (front-back).
    Centre zeroFaders that go either way (the amounts, SEMI, FINE, the warp points) are zero in the middle, with a small dead zone.
    PickupAfter a page change a fader does nothing until it reaches (or passes) the stored value; the same after a knob or this page changes it. A dashed line on the on-screen fader shows where a waiting 8mu fader is.
    LEDsEach fader's setting: how far it is from zero, or which choice for one that picks from a list. A mutant's depth and mix show what they are after modulation, and each LFO's rate fader pulses with it.
    TiltPast about 15° from flat, as the MOD page's last two faders set.

    Panel

    Switch upMain pitch (C1 to C7), X mutant 1 depth, Y mutant 2 depth
    Switch middleMain pitch, X mutant 1 ratio, Y mutant 2 ratio
    Switch downHold it: Main the wave, X mutant 1 mode, Y mutant 2 mode. The LEDs show the modes in binary (mutant 1 down the left, mutant 2 down the right)
    KnobsX and Y set the same values as the 8mu's faders. After the switch moves, or something else changes the value, a knob does nothing until it reaches it; that setting's box above has a dotted outline until then, and the Computer's bottom right LED blinks fast
    LEDsTop four: the 8mu page's button (lit for its first page, blinking for its second), or with no 8mu or editor, the two depths, sync and restart. Bottom two: the LFOs

    How it connects

    The card picks its USB mode at power-up. 8mu plugged into the card: the card reads it directly and this page isn't used. Computer plugged into the card: the card appears as a MIDI device called Mutagen, and an 8mu plugged into this computer is passed on by this page. After plugging a computer in, power-cycle the module.
    Help
    • Use a USB-C data cable, and close any other app using the card or the 8mu (Serial Monitor, a DAW).
    • Use Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser. Safari and iOS browsers can't do WebMIDI with SysEx.
    • Press Connect card & 8mu and allow MIDI with SysEx when the browser asks.
    • On-screen faders: drag, or focus one and use the arrow keys. Double-click puts one back to zero (the centre, for one that goes either way).
    • Several 8mus on this computer all drive the page together. With another Workshop editor open too (CVSeq, Weights, WaveSeq), one 8mu drives whichever editor you last clicked in; the header shows 8mu: here or 8mu: elsewhere, and clicking it takes the 8mu back.
    • The card reports how long it takes to make each sample. If a patch is too much for it (both mutants part-wet, windowed, with feedback, Out 2 on mutant 1…) it goes lean: Out 1 made once a sample instead of twice, a little rougher at high pitch. The line under Now playing says so.
