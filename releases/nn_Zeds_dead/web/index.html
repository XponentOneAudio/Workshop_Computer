    Inputs & outputs

    Inputs
    Audio In 1Left input; feeds both channels if In 2 is empty
    Audio In 2Right input
    CV In 1Cutoff, 1 V/oct, added to Main (calibrated from the module’s EEPROM)
    CV In 2Morph, added to X; 5 V sweeps the whole bank
    Pulse In 1, 2Not used
    Outputs
    Audio Out 1Left, filtered
    Audio Out 2Right, filtered
    Controls
    MainCutoff, 20 Hz to 18 kHz
    XMorph through the slots in use
    YResonance
    SwitchUp: bank A. Middle: bank B. Down (tap): stereo spread linked → cutoff → morph → both
    LEDsTop: output levels. Lower four: morph position; all glow while auditioning

    How it connects

    Plug the module into this computer by USB. The card appears as a MIDI device called MTMComputer; press Connect card and allow MIDI access (Chrome or Edge).

    Send to card copies both banks over; they play at once but are forgotten at power-off until you press Save to flash. Saving does not interrupt the audio.

    Audio load is measured on the card: the share of each sample’s time the audio core is busy. Keep the peak under about 90 %; more pairs cost more.
