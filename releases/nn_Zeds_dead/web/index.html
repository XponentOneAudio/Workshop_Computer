What do these numbers mean?

Pole Hz and Q are the natural frequency and quality factor of the resonance, as on an analogue filter, at the current preview cutoff (Q is shown with the Resonance knob at zero). A heavily damped pole (low Q) rings a little below its natural frequency, so on the z-plane it sits at a lower angle than its Hz value suggests. On the card everything is relative to the cutoff, so the whole shape slides up and down with the Main knob and CV 1. The Q stays the same as it moves.

Res (0–1) sets how much the Resonance knob sharpens this pole. A 6-pole lowpass usually lets only its last pair "sing".

Zeros: Lowpass, Highpass and Bandpass put real zeros at Nyquist (z = −1), at DC (z = +1), or one at each. These stay put when the cutoff moves. Notch / pair is a mirror pair that moves with the cutoff; radius 1 gives a perfect notch. Real lets you place two zeros anywhere on the axis. None gives a pure resonance.
