Faders

Probabilities

Each slider's chance of being drawn, as each voice sees it: voice 1 solid, voice 2 outlined. When voice 2 shares a page the two are the same. Click a chart to edit its page.

Presets

    Presets live in this browser. Loading one sends it to the card if one is connected. The card itself keeps nothing over power-off.

    Inputs & outputs

    Inputs
    Pulse In 1Clock. Each step is one beat, divided or multiplied by X (switch up). Followed from the second pulse; without one, or four periods (at least 2 s) after it stops, the card keeps its own 120 BPM
    OthersNot used
    Outputs
    CV Out 1, 2Pitch of voice 1, 2: 1 V/oct, C at 0 V, −2 V to about +6 V, slewed by Y
    Audio Out 1, 2Envelope of voice 1, 2, 0 to 5 V (uncalibrated), retriggered by every hit
    Pulse Out 1, 2Gate of voice 1, 2

    8mu

    The 8mu can plug into the module (no computer needed) or into this computer, which passes it on to the card. Its eight faders are relative probabilities: a fader at zero is never chosen, one twice as high as another twice as often. Buttons are acted on when you let go.

    ANotes of the scale. Scales with fewer than eight notes ignore the extra faders. Again: Notes 2
    BOctaves, −2 V to +5 V. Again: Octaves 2
    CGates: 0% (a rest), evenly up to 100%, which is tied into the next note without a new envelope. Again: Gates 2
    DRatchets: faders 1-4 play 1×, 1.5×, 2×, 3× the step on one note; faders 5-8 the same speeds, drawing a new note, octave and gate at every hit. Again: Ratchets 2
    Second pagesVoice 2's. While every fader on one is at zero, voice 2 draws from the first page, as a second line with the same probabilities; raise any fader and that page is voice 2's own. Each pair is separate.
    PickupAfter a page change a fader does nothing until it reaches (or passes) the stored value. While it waits, a dashed line on its on-screen fader shows where it is.
    LEDsFor 3 s after a fader or button is touched, the probabilities (the ratchets flash their speeds in time); then what is playing: the note or octave chosen, a bar to the gate length (the first LED dim for a rest), the ratchet flashing at each hit. On a first page voice 2 shows dimmer while it shares it.

    Panel

    Switch upMain scale (32), X clock ÷8 to ×8, Y slew on the pitch (off, then 1 ms to 2 s)
    Switch middleMain decay (5 ms to 5 s), X attack (0.5 ms to 1 s), Y pluck: from a straight fall to a fast drop with a long tail
    Switch downNothing; the knobs keep the settings of where it came from
    Knob pickupAfter the switch moves, a knob does nothing until it reaches its setting's value; the setting's box above has a dotted outline until it does
    LEDs1-4 the page (blinking for a second page) with an 8mu or this page, otherwise the gates and envelopes; the scale's number in binary (with LED 5) for a second when it changes. 5 flashes each step, 6 is lit with an 8mu or this page and blinks while a knob is waiting

    How it connects

    The card picks its USB mode at power-up. 8mu plugged into the card: the card reads it directly and this page isn't used. Computer plugged into the card: the card appears as a MIDI device called Guided Wander, and any 8mu plugged into this computer is passed on by this page. After plugging a computer in, power-cycle the module.
    Help
    • Use Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser. Safari and iOS browsers can't do WebMIDI with SysEx.
    • Press Connect card & 8mu and allow MIDI with SysEx when the browser asks.
    • Every 8mu on this computer drives the page together; their LEDs all follow it.
    • With the WaveSeq or CVSeq editor open too, one 8mu drives whichever editor you last clicked in. The header shows 8mu: here or 8mu: elsewhere; click it, or anywhere on the page, to take it. Use separate windows side by side rather than tabs: Chrome slows a background tab.
    • On-screen faders: drag, or focus one and use the arrow keys. Double-click sets one to zero.
    • Without a card the page still edits, previews and keeps presets, playing on its own at 120 BPM in the scale you choose.
