what.three.wiggles

Looking for the card…

Type three words, and the card builds its oscillators, LFO, random walk and gate patterns from them. The same words always make the same patch.

///
Any three words work. A what3words address gives you the sound of that place.
Connect the Workshop Computer by USB, with the what.three.wiggles card inserted.
Panel guide
Audio 1Drone: three operators in a feedback FM loop (switch middle) or ring modulated (switch up). One operator per word.
Audio 2Additive oscillator, partials set by the words. V/oct into CV In 1, sampled and held by triggers into CV In 2 (tracks continuously if CV In 2 is empty).
CV 1Random walk through the scale, with random holds and glides.
CV 2Complex LFO, about ±5 V.
Pulse 1 / 2Random triggers / flip-flop gates toggled by them.
Audio In 1Adds to the FM depth (ring mod amount).
Pulse In 1 / 2External clock (replaces the internal one) / reset.
Page AMain: FM depth · X: quantiser scale · Y: clock rate
Page BMain: CV 1 range · X: trigger density · Y: LFO rate
Switch downTap to change knob page (LED 4 lit on page B). Hold for a second to reset. LED 5 blinks until a knob catches up with its value on the new page.
